Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

This policy explains how TikoLumi Kids Uploader handles information when connecting to Google and YouTube.

Purpose

The application is a private tool used only by its owner to upload and manage original videos on the owner's YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

Information accessed

After the owner grants permission, the application may access the Google account identity required for authorization and YouTube channel data needed to upload videos and manage those uploads.

Use of information

Information is used only to authenticate the owner, upload requested videos, and perform channel-management actions initiated by the owner.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored as encrypted repository secrets and are never committed to public source code. The application does not sell, rent, or share Google user data with third parties.

Retention and deletion

Authorization credentials are retained only while the automation is in use. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings, after which stored credentials may be deleted.

Google API Services

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

Questions may be sent to the support address displayed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
